LUMORA V10 SIGNAL STABLE - HISTORY + NEWS COUNTDOWN FIX

FIXES
1. Signal History keeps completed WIN/LOSS records in data.json.
2. The server reconciles ALL expired signals that do not yet have a result whenever /api/v1/state or MT5 market data arrives.
3. A late EA result is deduplicated and attached to the original signal.
4. Live signal card clears old signal values after expiry/close.
5. News Countdown is now a live server-time countdown and does not stay frozen at 15m 42s.
6. News countdown is based on the next scheduled 30-minute window until a real calendar feed is connected.
7. Service-worker cache version was bumped so the updated page is loaded.

IMPORTANT
The current News Countdown is a deterministic dashboard timer, not a live economic-calendar feed. It should not be treated as a verified economic-news event time until a real news-calendar API/feed is connected.

START
